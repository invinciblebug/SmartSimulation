## About Smart Simulation

Smart Simulation is an intelligent Sims 4 mod designed for faster, smoother, and more stable gameplay with safe simulation optimizations. It helps improve simulation responsiveness, reduce stalls, and keep gameplay feeling smooth without changing the core experience.
